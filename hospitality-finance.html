Common questions about hospitality accounting

Can you provide management accounts for pubs, restaurants and cafés?

Yes. Reporting can cover sales, gross profit, staffing costs, overheads and cash flow, with frequency and individual venue analysis agreed for each client.

Can you help a hospitality group understand the profitability of each site?

Yes. Where suitable source records are available, we can report performance by location, compare budgets with actual results and highlight cost and margin trends.

Do you also support payroll, VAT and statutory accounts?

Yes. These can be included within an agreed accounting and compliance engagement or quoted separately. We agree responsibilities, deadlines and fees before work begins.
