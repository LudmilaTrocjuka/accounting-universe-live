Common questions about travel business accounting

Can you help a travel agency reconcile bookings, supplier payments and commissions?

Yes. We can agree a reporting and reconciliation process covering customer receipts, supplier invoices, prepayments, refunds, commission income and outstanding balances. The scope depends on the agency's booking model and available records.

Does every UK travel agency have to use the Tour Operators’ Margin Scheme (TOMS)?

No. VAT treatment depends on the actual contractual role of the business in each transaction, including whether it acts as a disclosed intermediary, undisclosed intermediary or principal. TOMS is not automatic simply because a business works in travel. See HMRC guidance for travel agents. We review the facts and agree any specialist VAT work before proceeding.

Can you help with foreign-currency payments and delayed travel commissions?

Yes. We offer multi-currency reconciliations, commission receivables tracking and management reporting to help travel businesses understand expected and received income.
