Could your business save on employer National Insurance?

Qualifying tips and discretionary service charges may be exempt from NIC where the relevant conditions are met, including genuinely independent allocation. Mandatory service charges distributed to staff remain subject to NIC. Tips cannot count towards minimum wage. We assess your actual arrangements and estimate potential savings; savings are not guaranteed and workers’ entitlement to tips must be protected.

HMRC guidance on tips and troncs (E24)

Prepare for 1 January 2027

Employers covered by the tipping legislation will need to consult affected workers or their representatives before introducing or revising a tipping policy, document feedback, share an anonymised summary and review the policy at least every three years. We can help prepare the financial records and consultation materials; the employer remains responsible for conducting meaningful consultation and meeting its legal duties.
