Full-service accounting & reporting checklist

Explore the reports, returns and compliance support available to UK companies. The services you need will depend on your business and its reporting obligations.

Annual accounts & financial statements
  • Annual statutory accounts preparation and filing
  • Profit & Loss (P&L) Statements
  • Balance Sheets / Statements of Financial Position
  • Notes to the Financial Statements
  • Directors’ Reports, where required
  • Cash Flow Statements, where applicable
  • Trial Balance and General Ledger Reports
  • Fixed Asset Registers and Depreciation Schedules
  • Accruals, Prepayments and Year-End Adjustments
  • Micro-Entity and Small Company Accounts, where eligible
Corporation Tax, VAT & HMRC
  • Corporation Tax Returns (CT600)
  • Corporation Tax Computations and Liability Calculations
  • Company Tax Return and supporting accounts submission to HMRC
  • Capital Allowances Calculations, where applicable
  • VAT Registration, VAT Returns and MTD Compliance
  • VAT Reconciliations and Transaction Reviews
  • HMRC Correspondence, Tax Queries and Compliance Support, where authorised
  • Self Assessment Tax Returns for Directors, where agreed separately
Companies House compliance
  • Annual Confirmation Statements (CS01)
  • Statutory Accounts Filing
  • Changes to Directors, Shareholders and Persons with Significant Control (PSC)
  • Registered Office and Company Information Updates
  • Share Capital and Relevant Statutory Filings
  • Monitoring of Filing Deadlines and Compliance Requirements
Payroll, RTI & employee reporting
  • Weekly and Monthly Payroll Processing and Reports
  • Payslips and Payroll Summaries
  • Full Payment Submissions (FPS)
  • Employer Payment Summaries (EPS), where required
  • P60 and P45 Preparation
  • P11D and P11D(b) Reporting, where applicable
  • PAYE and National Insurance Reconciliations
  • Workplace Pension Contributions and Auto-Enrolment Reporting
Monthly & quarterly management reporting
  • Monthly and Quarterly Profit & Loss Reports
  • Balance Sheet Reports and Management Accounts
  • Cash Flow Statements and Forecasts
  • Budgeting and Budget-versus-Actual Reports
  • Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable Reports
  • Aged Debtors and Aged Creditors Reports
  • Bank, Credit Card and Payment Platform Reconciliations
  • Director’s Loan Account Reports
  • Revenue, Expense and Profitability Analysis
  • Financial Performance and KPI Reports
Specialist business reporting
  • Hospitality Revenue, Cost and Margin Analysis
  • Restaurant and Pub Daily Sales Reconciliations
  • Travel Agency Commission and Supplier Reconciliations
  • Multi-Currency Transaction Reporting
  • E-Commerce Sales and Payment Platform Reconciliations
  • Inventory, Stock Valuation and Cost of Sales Reports
  • Construction Industry Scheme (CIS) Returns, where applicable

One accounting partner. Complete financial oversight. Reporting requirements and service scope vary by business. Exact inclusions, reporting frequency and fees are agreed individually in writing; specialist services and statutory filings may be quoted separately.
