Cookie Policy

We remember cookie preferences where required for site functionality. For website statistics we use Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use analytics cookies.

If you select Accept all, we also load Metricool to measure website visits and page views. Metricool is not loaded if you reject non-essential cookies or have not made a choice.

Managing cookies

You can change your browser settings to block cookies. Our banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies.
